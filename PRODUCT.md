# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Customers browsing a broad product catalog on mobile and desktop, looking to compare options and decide what to buy.

## Product Purpose
Provide a single, organized place to discover categories and products, scan quickly, and reach confident purchase decisions.

## Brand Personality
Warm, curated, eye-catching yet minimal.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards, neon colorways, and overdone decorative effects.

## Design Principles
1. Curated discovery over cluttered abundance.
2. Confidence through clarity, not noise.
3. Playful moments with restraint.
4. Fast scanning for busy shoppers.
5. Consistent, trustworthy presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA, provide high contrast, respect reduced motion, and use animation only where it supports understanding.
